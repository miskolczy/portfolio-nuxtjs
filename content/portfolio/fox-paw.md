Paw was Revendo’s first design system, created specifically for Fox, our inventory management and point-of-sale (POS) system. Fox connects nearly every part of the business, from our physical stores and online shop to our warehouse.

I led the design of Fox from day one. I started by defining how the product should be structured: which teams would use it and what each one needed to see. Fox was built on two core ideas. The first was entities, the key things staff work with, displayed in tables that are easy to scan and act on. The second was flows, which guide staff through the system step by step.

Together with the product owner, I refined and shaped new processes around the people using Fox and the business behind it. Out of this structure grew Paw, our design system. Every component had to earn its place, which kept the system lean and easy to build with.
